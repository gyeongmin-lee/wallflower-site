Wallflower

Privacy Policy

Effective November 3, 2026

Wallflower (the “App”) has no accounts. It does not ask for your name, email address, or phone number, and your cards never leave your device. The App sends only the two kinds of information below off your device, and both are linked only to a random ID that cannot identify you.

1. Information Sent Off Your Device and Why

PurposeInformation
Verifying and restoring Pro purchasesRandom app user ID, purchase history (product, transaction ID, and purchase, renewal, and expiration times), country and currency, app and iOS version
Usage statistics to improve the App and fix errorsRandom ID, app usage records (which screens you opened and which buttons you tapped), your answers to the questions asked when you first open the App (where you keep the sentences that stay with you, and whether you revisit them), error records when the App quits unexpectedly (where in the code it stopped), device information (model, screen size, time zone, network type), app and iOS version, language

Usage statistics never include the contents of your cards, your photos, or your IP address. Payments are handled by Apple, and the App never receives payment details such as credit card numbers.

2. Information Processed Only on Your Device

  • Cards and walls: Everything, including the photos you add to cards, is stored only on this device.
  • Camera: Used only to recognize sentences in a book or to take a photo for a card. Text recognition happens on the device.
  • Photos: The App reads only the photos you choose. It does not access your whole photo library.
  • Motion sensors: Used only to adjust the wall’s perspective as you turn your device, and never recorded.

3. Retention and Deletion

  • Purchase history is kept for as long as it is needed to verify Pro status.
  • Usage statistics are deleted one year after they are collected.
  • Information stored on your device is deleted when you delete the App.

4. Provision to Third Parties

We do not provide personal information to any third party.

5. Outsourced Processing and International Transfer

The information in Section 1 is processed on our behalf by the following companies. It is encrypted and sent to them over the internet while you use the App. Both companies protect personal information to the same standard as this policy.

6. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, deletion of, or suspension of processing of your personal information at any time. Send your request to the email address below and it will be handled without delay. However, because the App uses only a random ID, we may not be able to find your records.

7. Security Measures

  • The App receives only the minimum information it needs.
  • Everything sent is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).
  • Only the Privacy Officer can access the processors’ dashboards, and only with two-factor authentication.

8. Automatic Collection

The App does not use cookies. The random ID is stored within the App and is deleted when you delete the App. The App does not use the advertising identifier (IDFA) and does not track you across other companies’ apps or websites.
