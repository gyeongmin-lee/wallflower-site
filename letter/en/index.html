Where Your Subscription Goes

Hi there!

I’m cozyco, and I make wallflower on my own. No team, no company, just me!

This is the wall that started it all. When I was studying abroad in Canada, I covered a corkboard in my room with sticky notes, one for every line that stuck with me. On lonely nights, I’d read them. Some lines landed differently depending on my mood, and some were exactly what I needed to hear. That wall kept me going. I made wallflower so you could have a wall like that too, one you can open anywhere.

Your subscription keeps that wall growing. Here’s how.

What your subscription does

  • More templates, regularly. The kind that make you smile just by looking at them.
  • Licensed fonts, illustrations, and quotes. Good ones cost money, and your support lets me bring them in without second-guessing.
  • The features you ask for. I build them one at a time, and only when they make your reading time richer.

My promise to you 🌼

  1. Regular updates with new features and templates.
  2. I read every bit of feedback, and I build what’s truly needed first.

Not a subscription person? There’s a one-time lifetime option too.
